The Primary Financial Drain

Tables alone generated -$17,725 in net deficit, followed by persistent losses in Bookcases. While Technology products acted as major cash cows, furniture lines severely eroded portfolio margins.

The Root Mechanism: Discount Distortion

Transactional cross-tabulation reveals that products sold with discounts above 20% consistently generated negative margins, heavily compounded by high logistics overheads on bulky items.

Superstore Business Audit Report Slide 03 / 04
Strategic Roadmap
Remediation Strategies & Management Next Steps
Preserving margins without compromising top-line enterprise growth

1. Enforce Strict Promotional Ceilings

Cap maximum discretionary discounts on Tables and Bookcases at 12% to 15%. Eliminating transactions with >20% discounts immediately recovers an estimated +$28,000 to +$35,000 in bottom-line margin.

2. Regional Freight & Pricing Restructuring

Audit regional delivery contracts in deficit-heavy territories like Texas (-$25.7K) and Ohio (-$16.9K). Impose minimum order values or pass-through shipping surcharges on heavy furniture items.

3. Capital Reallocation to Cash Cows

Shift marketing acquisition budgets toward high-margin sub-categories, particularly Copiers (EBITDA Margin >35%), Phones, and Accessories, where promo elasticity generates positive net returns.

4. B2B Corporate Contract Optimization

Transition Corporate and Home Office furniture buyers from standard retail markdowns to volume-committed service contracts, protecting gross margin through structured tiers rather than flat discounts.
